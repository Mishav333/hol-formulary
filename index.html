The Formulary.

Every room in the House, and what it holds.

Reading the House

One house. Four rooms. One formulary.

Everything the House is licensed to prescribe sits inside one of four rooms: the Weight Room, the Cosmetic Quarter, the Private Room, and the Recovery Wing. A member walks into the room they need, at the time in life they need it.

Several items sit naturally in more than one room. Where that is true, it is tagged and shown in both. Every description below explains what a therapy does and how it works. It does not promise an outcome, and it is not a substitute for a prescriber's own assessment.

Weight · Room 01

The Weight Room

Metabolic and hormonal care, prescriber led. For the season when the scales stop answering to effort alone.

5-Amino-1MQ

Oral capsule · NNMT inhibitor

Inhibits the NNMT enzyme, preserving the body's NAD+ pool to support mitochondrial activity and fat metabolism at the cellular level.

AOD-9604

Cream or injection · HGH fragment (176-191)

A modified fragment of human growth hormone isolated for its role in fat metabolism, without the broader growth-hormone signalling of the full molecule.

Infinity BF Shot

IV shot · taurine, acetyl-L-carnitine

A lipotropic combination that supports the transport of fatty acids into the mitochondria for energy use, taken alongside a broader weight program.

Infinity SD Shot

IV shot · methionine, inositol, choline, carnitine, methylcobalamin

A classic lipotropic blend supporting fat metabolism and liver-level processing of dietary fat.

Mounjaro

Tirzepatide · dual GIP/GLP-1 receptor agonist

A TGA-approved dual-incretin agonist, engaging both GIP and GLP-1 receptors. Supports appetite regulation and glycaemic control alongside a prescriber-directed metabolic plan.

A TGA-approved GLP-1 receptor agonist, originally indicated for type 2 diabetes management and used under prescriber direction to support appetite and metabolic control.

SARMS S-22

Injection or cartridge · selective androgen receptor modulator

A tissue-selective androgen receptor modulator, engaging muscle and bone androgen receptors while sparing the broader hormonal footprint of anabolic steroids.

SLU-PP-332

Oral capsule · ERRα/β/γ agonist

An exercise-mimetic compound that activates the ERR family of metabolic receptors, supporting fatty-acid oxidation and mitochondrial function consistent with an aerobic training response.

TRT · Testosterone Cypionate, Enanthate & Primoteston Depot

Injection, various esters and carrier oils

Prescriber-directed testosterone replacement for confirmed low testosterone, supporting energy, body composition and the broader metabolic picture that hormone decline affects.

Tesamorelin

Injection or cartridge · GHRH analogue

A growth hormone releasing hormone analogue studied specifically for its effect on visceral fat, the metabolically active fat carried around the abdomen.

Wegovy

Semaglutide · GLP-1 receptor agonist

A TGA-approved GLP-1 receptor agonist. Slows gastric emptying and acts on appetite-regulating centres in the brain, supporting sustained reduction in food intake under prescriber supervision.
